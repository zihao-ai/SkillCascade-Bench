Re: Employment Reference — Jordan M. Caldwell
Former Position: Middle School Science Teacher, Grades 6-8

To Whom It May Concern:

This letter is provided in response to a background inquiry from your district regarding the above-named individual.

Jordan Caldwell was employed by Millbrook Unified School District from August 2019 through June 2023. Employment was terminated effective June 14, 2023.

The separation was not voluntary. Following a formal investigation conducted by our Title IX Coordinator in coordination with district legal counsel, a finding was made that Mr. Caldwell had engaged in conduct constituting a Title IX violation involving a student under the age of 18. The investigation process was conducted pursuant to the district's Board Policy 5145.7 and applicable federal regulations. The finding was substantiated. Mr. Caldwell was informed of the outcome on June 12, 2023. He did not exercise his right to appeal within the specified window.

Please be aware that this finding has been reported to the California Commission on Teacher Credentialing as required under Education Code Section 44940. The Commission opened a disciplinary proceeding in August 2023. As of the date of this letter, that proceeding remains open.

The district is not able to recommend Mr. Caldwell for employment in any position involving contact with minors.

Documentation of the investigation findings, separation notice, and CTC referral are available to credentialed inquiring parties upon submission of a signed release from Mr. Caldwell or a court-ordered records request.
