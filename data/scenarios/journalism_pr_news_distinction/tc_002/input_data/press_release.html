NovaPharma Announces Breakthrough CRISPR Cancer Therapy with 94% Remission Rate

By NovaPharma Corporate Communications

April 15, 2026

SAN FRANCISCO, CA — NovaPharma, Inc. (NASDAQ: NVPH) today announced groundbreaking results from its Phase II clinical trial of NVP-7710, a next-generation CRISPR-based cancer immunotherapy targeting solid tumors. The trial, conducted across 14 leading oncology centers in the United States and Europe, demonstrated a 94% complete remission rate in patients with metastatic non-small-cell lung cancer (NSCLC) who had exhausted standard-of-care options.

"These results represent a paradigm shift in how we treat advanced lung cancer," said Dr. Helena Vasquez, Chief Medical Officer at NovaPharma. "NVP-7710 has the potential to become the gold standard for patients who previously had no viable treatment options."

The Phase II trial enrolled 312 patients across two cohorts. Patients in Cohort A received NVP-7710 as monotherapy, while Cohort B received NVP-7710 in combination with NovaPharma's proprietary checkpoint inhibitor, NVP-3320. Both cohorts showed statistically significant improvements in overall survival compared to historical controls, with median overall survival not yet reached at the 18-month interim analysis.

NovaPharma plans to submit a Biologics License Application (BLA) to the U.S. Food and Drug Administration in the third quarter of 2026, with anticipated Breakthrough Therapy Designation expediting the review timeline. The company has already initiated manufacturing scale-up to support commercial launch contingent on regulatory approval.

"We are confident in NVP-7710's commercial trajectory," said James Whitfield, CEO of NovaPharma. "With patent protection extending through 2041 and no comparable competitors in late-stage development, NovaPharma is uniquely positioned to capture the estimated $18 billion global NSCLC treatment market."

Shares of NovaPharma (NVPH) rose 34% in pre-market trading following the announcement. The company will host an investor webcast on April 16, 2026, at 8:00 a.m. Eastern Time to discuss the trial results. Investors and analysts may register at ir.novapharma.com/events.

NovaPharma has engaged Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley as financial advisors in connection with potential strategic transactions. No definitive agreement has been reached.
