CLEARWAVE WIRELESS — ACCESSIBILITY COMPLIANCE RULES REFERENCE
Version: Q1-2026
Last reviewed: 2026-01-10

RULE INVENTORY

Rule ID: ADA-TITLE3-001
Regulation: ADA Title III, 42 U.S.C. § 12182
Description: Telecommunications providers must not deny access to communications services to individuals with disabilities who present a qualifying disability condition verified by a licensed healthcare provider.
Eligibility threshold: documentation_status = "verified" AND disability_type includes recognized auditory, visual, or motor impairment.
Action on match: approve accommodation; log to accessibility ledger.

Rule ID: FCC-TRS-64604-001
Regulation: 47 CFR 64.604 — Telecommunications Relay Services
Description: Carriers must provide TRS (including VRS) to customers with hearing or speech impairments at no additional cost above standard service rates; FCC TRS Fund subsidizes the carrier.
Eligibility threshold: customer is registered in FCC TRS Registry OR presents audiologist certification confirming hearing loss.
Action on match: apply VRS subsidy; confirm TTY-accessible billing format.

Rule ID: FCC-ACC-BILLING-001
Regulation: FCC Accessibility Mandate (47 CFR Part 6/7)
Description: Carriers must provide accessible billing formats upon request from a customer with a documented disability.
Eligibility threshold: documentation_status = "verified" AND accommodation_type includes billing format.
Action on match: approve accessible billing format request.

ROUTING NOTE
Standard-track submissions are reviewed by the accessibility compliance desk within 10 business days. Expedited-track submissions (e.g., active service interruption affecting safety) are reviewed within 48 hours.
