PHASE III RANDOMIZED CONTROLLED TRIAL

Title: Efficacy and Safety of Velatinib versus Placebo in Patients with Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC) Following Platinum-Based Chemotherapy: The VELOCE-3 Trial

Journal: The Lancet Oncology (Accepted 2026-03-15)
DOI: 10.1016/S1470-2045(26)00147-X

AUTHORS
Harrington MK, Okonkwo T, Sato Y, et al.

ABSTRACT
Background: Velatinib, a selective FGFR2/3 inhibitor, showed activity in preclinical NSCLC models. We assessed its efficacy as maintenance therapy.

Methods: VELOCE-3 was a double-blind, placebo-controlled phase III trial. Adults with stage IIIB/IV NSCLC who had not progressed after 4–6 cycles of platinum doublet chemotherapy were randomly assigned (1:1) to velatinib 400 mg orally once daily or matching placebo. The primary endpoint was overall survival (OS). Secondary endpoints included progression-free survival (PFS) and objective response rate (ORR).

Findings: 1,247 patients were enrolled. Median OS was 18.3 months in the velatinib group versus 15.7 months in the placebo group (HR 0.85; 95% CI 0.75–0.97; p=0.018). ORR was 80/624 (12.8%) versus 44/623 (7.1%) (OR 1.93; p=0.0007). Median PFS was 6.4 vs 4.1 months (HR 0.71; 95% CI 0.62–0.81; p<0.0001).

Grade 3–4 adverse events included hyperphosphataemia (14.3%), fatigue (11.9%), and nail changes (5.0%).

Interpretation: Velatinib demonstrated a statistically significant improvement in OS compared with placebo, meeting the pre-specified significance boundary (p<0.025).

Conclusions: Velatinib as maintenance therapy provided a statistically significant OS improvement. Regulatory review is pending.
