8.3 Adverse Reactions

The following adverse reactions have been identified during Phase 1 and Phase 2 clinical studies with STUDY001 compound.

Based on Phase 1 data (N=45 treated):
- Nausea: commonly reported and generally mild in severity.
- Headache: commonly reported and generally mild in severity.
- Fatigue: commonly reported; most cases resolved without intervention.

Hepatic findings were not observed in Phase 1. Updated data from the ongoing Phase 2 trial (interim cutoff 2025-06-30) should be incorporated into v5.0 of this document.
