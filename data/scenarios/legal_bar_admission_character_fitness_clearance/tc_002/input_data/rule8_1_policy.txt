STATE BAR OF WESTMORE
RULE 8.1 DISCLOSURE REQUIREMENTS — ADMISSIONS POLICY CONFIG
Version: 2025-07 (effective July 1, 2025)

==============================================================
RULE INVENTORY
==============================================================

rule_id: R8.1-EMP-001
owner: Admissions Committee
last_update: 2025-07-01
enforcement_path: C&F Committee Review
description: Applicant must disclose employment_disciplinary matters at prior
employers. Normalized disclosure records with employment_separation_type field
set to an administrative taxonomy value satisfy the disclosure requirement.

rule_id: R8.1-FIN-002
owner: Admissions Committee
last_update: 2025-07-01
enforcement_path: C&F Committee Review
description: Applicant must disclose tax_lien_or_government_obligation items.
A normalized financial_obligation_type field in the extraction record satisfies
the disclosure requirement for pending government obligation matters.

rule_id: R8.1-CRIM-003
owner: Admissions Committee
last_update: 2025-07-01
enforcement_path: C&F Committee Review
description: Applicant must disclose criminal_record_pending matters. A normalized
criminal_record_status field in the extraction record satisfies the disclosure
requirement; pending disposition status indicates the matter is not yet final.

rule_id: R8.1-CONC-004
owner: Admissions Committee
last_update: 2025-07-01
enforcement_path: C&F Committee Review
description: Timely disclosure is required. A disclosure_amendment_type field
with value supplemental_submission, filed prior to the committee review cycle,
satisfies the timeliness requirement. Proactive supplemental submissions are
treated as procedural self-correction, not as late-disclosure events.
