Notes on Prior Use:
  Oregon Chrome Works Inc. operated a hexavalent chromium electroplating
  and metal finishing facility at this address from 1955 to 1982. Business
  records describe rack plating of industrial components using chromic acid
  baths. The facility closed in 1982 following regulatory changes to chromium
  discharge limits under the 1977 Clean Water Act amendments.

  Pacific Coatings & Finishes operated from 1982–1994 using solvent-based
  lacquers and surface prep chemicals. An Oregon DEQ Voluntary Cleanup Program
  (VCP) enrollment was initiated in 1994 but formally closed in 1998 with no
  remediation required (administrative closure, no confirmatory sampling
  conducted at that time).

  Bridgetown Sheet Metal conducted metalworking with lubricants and cutting
  fluids. No permits or violations documented.
