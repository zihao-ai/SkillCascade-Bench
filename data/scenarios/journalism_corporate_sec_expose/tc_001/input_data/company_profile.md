# NovaBio Therapeutics — Company Profile

NovaBio Therapeutics (NASDAQ: NBIO) is a mid-cap pharmaceutical company headquartered in Cambridge, MA, focused on oncology and rare disease biologics. The company has been under shareholder scrutiny following two consecutive years of executive compensation increases that outpaced revenue growth on a percentage basis.

## Key Facts for Investigative Context

- **CEO**: Dr. Margaret Holloway (tenure: 7 years)
- **Board Compensation Committee Chair**: James R. Whitfield
- **Institutional Shareholder Services (ISS) Rating**: Against — pay-for-performance misalignment flagged in proxy advisory report
- **Say-on-Pay vote (2025 AGM)**: 61% approval (down from 78% in 2024)
- **Peer group median CEO pay**: $9.2M (as disclosed in DEF 14A)
- **Pipeline**: 3 Phase 3 trials, 2 NDA submissions pending
