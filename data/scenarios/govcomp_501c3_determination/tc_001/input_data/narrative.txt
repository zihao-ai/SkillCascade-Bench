HOPE FORWARD FOUNDATION — FORM 1023 NARRATIVE STATEMENT
Section IV: Description of Activities

Hope Forward Foundation is a nonprofit corporation organized exclusively for charitable,
educational, and scientific purposes under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code.
The Foundation's primary mission is to provide transitional housing support, job-readiness
workshops, and mental-health counseling services to unhoused adults in the greater
Riverside County area.

PROGRAM SERVICES DESCRIPTION

Our flagship program, "StepUp Riverside," delivered $892,000 in direct charitable services
during the most recently completed fiscal year. These services included:
  - 1,840 nights of transitional shelter provided at no cost to clients
  - 312 individual career counseling sessions with licensed social workers
  - 48 employer-partnership job placement events benefiting 189 participants
  - Weekly mental-health support groups facilitated by LCSW-supervised volunteers

Administrative and overhead functions accounted for $108,000, covering essential
management, accounting, and regulatory compliance activities necessary to maintain
organizational integrity and accountability to donors.

The Foundation is governed by a seven-member volunteer Board of Directors with no
compensation. The Executive Director's salary is benchmarked to comparable nonprofits
at the 50th percentile of the regional market. No board member, officer, or major
donor receives any private benefit from Foundation activities beyond reasonable
reimbursement of documented out-of-pocket expenses.

FUTURE PLANS

Over the next three years, Hope Forward Foundation intends to expand its StepUp Riverside
program to a second site in San Bernardino County, contingent upon securing additional
grant funding. We do not anticipate any material change in the ratio of program expenses
to total organizational expenditures.

Executive Director, Hope Forward Foundation
