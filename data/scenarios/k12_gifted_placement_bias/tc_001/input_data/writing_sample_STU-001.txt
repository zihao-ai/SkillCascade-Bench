I think the water cycle is very important for the earth. The water goes up to the sky and then it comes back down as rain. The rain goes to the rivers and the rivers go to the ocean. Without water, everything would die. The water goes up again and makes clouds. Clouds make rain and rain makes the rivers full. I think the water cycle is good because it helps the earth to have water always. The water is always moving and this is good for the earth.
