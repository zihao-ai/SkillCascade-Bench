FIRST NOTICE OF LOSS — CYBER LIABILITY CLAIM
Policy Number: CYB-2025-00847
Insured: Meridian Staffing Solutions, LLC
Incident Date: March 14, 2026
Report Date: March 28, 2026

INCIDENT DESCRIPTION

On March 14, 2026, Meridian Staffing Solutions discovered that an employee had responded to a phishing email impersonating our IT department. The employee provided their Active Directory credentials through a fake login portal. Using those credentials, an unauthorized third party gained access to the employee's email account and the HR document repository to which the account had access.

Preliminary investigation indicates that the unauthorized access may have been limited to browsing activity within the HR repository. The repository contains employee records. The scope of records that may have been exposed or viewed by the unauthorized party is currently under investigation; we cannot rule out that a subset of employee records were accessed. Our internal security team estimates that approximately 200 employee personnel files were potentially accessed, though we cannot confirm whether any data was extracted.

We have reset the affected credentials, enabled multi-factor authentication on all accounts, and engaged a forensic firm to complete the investigation. Regulatory counsel is reviewing applicable breach notification obligations.

The HR document repository contained the following data fields: full name, home address, date of birth, Social Security Number, direct deposit bank routing and account numbers, W-2 tax data, and performance review notes.

We are submitting this first notice of loss under our cyber liability policy and requesting coverage determination and reserve guidance from the carrier.

Policy Limits: $2,000,000 per occurrence / $4,000,000 aggregate
Deductible: $25,000
Jurisdiction: State of Delaware, USA
