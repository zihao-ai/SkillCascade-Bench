Dear Members of the Admissions Committee,

I am writing to enthusiastically recommend Maria Elena Gutierrez for admission to your doctoral program in Computer Science. I have had the privilege of knowing Maria Elena for three years as her undergraduate thesis advisor at California State University, Los Angeles (Cal State LA), a Hispanic-Serving Institution serving a predominantly first-generation college student population.

Maria Elena is one of the most talented students I have mentored in fifteen years of teaching. Her background is remarkable: she grew up in a bilingual Spanish-English household in East Los Angeles and was the first in her family to attend a four-year university. Despite beginning her undergraduate career without the AP coursework and research exposure common at more resourced institutions, she has more than compensated through exceptional drive and intellectual ability.

Her undergraduate thesis, "Cross-lingual Transfer in Low-Resource NLP: A Case Study in Indigenous Mexican Languages," represents work of genuine scholarly originality. She identified a gap in the NLP literature regarding Nahuatl and Mixtec language modeling, assembled a dataset through collaboration with community elders and language documentation nonprofits, and demonstrated significant improvements in zero-shot transfer accuracy over the baseline. This work was accepted as a workshop paper at ACL 2025 — an extraordinary achievement for an undergraduate, particularly one at a primarily teaching-focused institution without a graduate research program.

Maria Elena's cultural background has directly enriched her research vision. Her lived experience as a Latina woman from an immigrant household gives her a perspective on language technology that most of her peers at elite research universities simply do not have. She has identified real-world problems that remain invisible to researchers operating primarily in English-language contexts, and she has the technical skill to address them rigorously.

I strongly recommend Maria Elena without reservation. Please feel free to contact me at professor_delgado@calstatela.edu or 323-555-0198.

Sincerely,

Professor Carlos Delgado
