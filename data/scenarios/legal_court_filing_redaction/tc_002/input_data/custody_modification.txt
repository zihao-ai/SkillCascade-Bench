I, Rachel Anne Kowalski, hereby declare under penalty of perjury under the laws of the State of Washington:

1. PARTIES AND MINOR CHILD

Petitioner Rachel Anne Kowalski and Respondent David Paul Kowalski are the parents of one minor child: the minor daughter, Sofia Claire Kowalski, age 7, born September 3, 2018. The child of the parties, Sofia Claire Kowalski, currently resides primarily with Petitioner at 1812 Fernwood Court, Renton, WA 98055, and is enrolled in second grade at Hazelwood Elementary School, 655 S 128th Street, Renton, WA 98057. Sofia Claire Kowalski has an Individual Education Program (IEP) on file with Hazelwood Elementary due to a diagnosed learning disability.

2. GROUNDS FOR MODIFICATION

Since entry of the original Parenting Plan on November 14, 2023, there has been a substantial change in circumstances warranting modification:

a. Respondent David Paul Kowalski relocated from Kent, WA to Issaquah, WA in January 2026, increasing the distance from the child's primary school to approximately 34 miles and rendering the current alternating-week schedule logistically unworkable for consistent school attendance.

b. On March 8, 2026, the minor child, Sofia Claire Kowalski, reported to her school counselor at Hazelwood Elementary that she felt unsafe during weekend exchanges with Respondent due to the presence of an unidentified adult male at Respondent's residence. The counselor's report (attached as Exhibit A) documents the child's statements and was submitted to King County DCYF.

c. On March 20, 2026, Respondent failed to return Sofia Claire Kowalski by the agreed exchange time of 6:00 p.m., instead returning her more than four hours late, at 10:15 p.m. on a school night. Sofia Claire Kowalski arrived without having eaten dinner and was visibly distressed. Petitioner documented this incident and filed a police report (King County Sheriff Report No. 2026-KCS-014477).

d. Petitioner's employer transferred her to a new work location in Tukwila, WA, effective February 1, 2026, which requires revised daily scheduling to ensure Sofia Claire Kowalski's timely drop-off and pickup at Hazelwood Elementary.

3. RELIEF REQUESTED

Petitioner respectfully requests that this Court:

a. Modify the existing Parenting Plan to designate Petitioner as the primary residential parent for the minor child, Sofia Claire Kowalski;
b. Establish a revised residential schedule providing Respondent with scheduled visitation on alternating weekends from Friday at 6:00 p.m. to Sunday at 6:00 p.m.;
c. Require that any exchange of the minor child, Sofia Claire Kowalski, take place at a neutral location designated by the Court;
d. Order a Guardian ad Litem investigation into the residential circumstances of both parties with specific attention to the minor child's welfare and educational continuity.
