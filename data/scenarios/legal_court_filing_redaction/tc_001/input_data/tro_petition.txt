I, Sandra Lynn Delray, being of full age, hereby certify and state:

1. PARTIES AND RELATIONSHIP

Petitioner Sandra Lynn Delray and Respondent Thomas James Delray are husband and wife, currently separated, and are parties to pending divorce proceedings in this Court. The marriage has produced one child, the minor child, Ethan Marcus Delray, age 9, born March 14, 2016. The child of the parties, Ethan Marcus Delray, currently resides with Petitioner at 47 Maple Crest Drive, Trenton, NJ 08611, and is enrolled at Lincoln Elementary School, 200 Academy Street, Trenton, NJ 08618.

2. MOST RECENT INCIDENT OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE

On April 15, 2026, at approximately 9:45 p.m., Respondent Thomas James Delray appeared unannounced at Petitioner's residence at 47 Maple Crest Drive. Respondent was visibly intoxicated. Respondent forced his way through the front door, pushed Petitioner against the wall, and shouted threatening language in the presence of the minor child, Ethan Marcus Delray, who was awakened by the disturbance and witnessed the incident from the stairwell.

Petitioner sustained bruising to her left shoulder. Petitioner immediately called 911. Trenton Police Department responded (Report No. 2026-TR-04892). Respondent was removed from the premises.

3. PRIOR HISTORY OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE

a. On February 3, 2026, Respondent struck Petitioner's vehicle with his fist in the parking lot of Trenton Family Court, causing damage and frightening the minor child, Ethan Marcus Delray, who was present in the vehicle.

b. On December 19, 2025, Respondent sent repeated text messages to Petitioner containing threats to "take the kid and disappear." These messages have been preserved and are attached as Exhibit A.

c. On October 7, 2025, Respondent appeared at Lincoln Elementary School, 200 Academy Street, Trenton, demanding to remove Ethan Marcus Delray from school without authorization. School staff contacted Petitioner and refused to release the child.

4. RELIEF REQUESTED

Petitioner respectfully requests that this Court enter a Temporary Restraining Order:

a. Prohibiting Respondent from having any contact with Petitioner;
b. Prohibiting Respondent from entering or approaching the residence at 47 Maple Crest Drive, Trenton, NJ 08611;
c. Prohibiting Respondent from approaching Lincoln Elementary School, 200 Academy Street, Trenton, NJ 08618;
d. Awarding temporary residential custody of the minor child, Ethan Marcus Delray, to Petitioner, pending final hearing;
e. Ordering Respondent to surrender any firearms in his possession.

5. CERTIFICATION

I certify that the foregoing statements are true. I am aware that if any of the foregoing statements made by me are willfully false, I am subject to punishment.
