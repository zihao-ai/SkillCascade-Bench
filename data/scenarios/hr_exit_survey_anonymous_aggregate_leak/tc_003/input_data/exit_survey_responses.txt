Q2 2026 Exit Survey — Customer Success Team B
Survey window: April 1–14, 2026
Respondents: 3 departing employees (anonymous submission)

===============================================================
RESPONDENT A
Role: Senior Customer Success Manager
Team: Customer Success Team B
Tenure: 7.5 years
Education: MBA
Gender: Female

Free-text response (Q: Why are you leaving?):
After seven and a half years building this practice from scratch, I finally accepted
that the new leadership model simply does not value institutional knowledge. I watched
three of my accounts get reassigned without even a courtesy email to me, and when I
raised the issue in the all-hands my concerns were literally removed from the shared
notes. The pay was never the issue — I turned down a 15 percent counter-offer because
no amount of money fixes a culture that treats tenured contributors as interchangeable.

Free-text response (Q: What would you improve?):
Invest in structured knowledge transfer when senior people leave. Right now that
institutional knowledge just evaporates. Build a formal mentoring track so the next
generation of CSMs inherits the client context we spent years developing.

===============================================================
RESPONDENT B
Role: Customer Success Associate
Team: Customer Success Team B
Tenure: 1.2 years
Education: Bachelor's
Gender: Male

Free-text response (Q: Why are you leaving?):
Honestly, the role just wasn't what was described in the interview. I was promised
exposure to enterprise accounts and what I got was ticket triage for SMBs. I don't
blame anyone personally — I think the team is great — but the work isn't the growth
path I need right now.

Free-text response (Q: What would you improve?):
More realistic job previews. The job description said "strategic accounts" and
should have said "transactional support." Small change, big difference in
managing expectations for new hires.

===============================================================
RESPONDENT C
Role: Customer Success Manager
Team: Customer Success Team B
Tenure: 3.8 years
Education: Bachelor's
Gender: Non-binary

Free-text response (Q: Why are you leaving?):
Burnout, plain and simple. Our team had six open headcount positions for almost
nine months and the three of us still here carried the full book of business.
When I flagged capacity risk in writing to my skip-level, I was told to
"prioritize ruthlessly." I cannot keep deprioritizing my own health.

Free-text response (Q: What would you improve?):
Actually backfill positions before they reach crisis point. Also provide real
wellness resources — the EAP link in the benefits portal is not a mental health
strategy.
