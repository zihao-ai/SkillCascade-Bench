INCIDENT DESCRIPTION
On March 4, 2026, Ms. Okonkwo was restocking overhead shelving in aisle 7 when a box shifted unexpectedly and struck her right shoulder and neck. She reported the incident to her floor manager the same day and was evaluated at an urgent care clinic. The treating provider diagnosed a cervical sprain and strain of the right trapezius and paraspinal muscles.

TREATMENT TO DATE
Ms. Okonkwo has attended six sessions of physical therapy over the past five weeks with modest improvement. As of the date of this report (several weeks post-injury), she continues to report persistent neck pain with radiation into the right shoulder and limited cervical range of motion. Her provider has documented ongoing soft-tissue guarding and has not cleared her for regular overhead lifting or prolonged stationary standing. She remains on restricted duty and has been unable to perform her standard cashier and shelving responsibilities.

DIAGNOSIS
