RESIDENTIAL PROPERTY INSPECTION REPORT
Property: 4217 Maplewood Drive, Cedar Falls, IA 50613
Inspection Date: March 10, 2026
Inspector: Tom Beckwith, ASHI Certified Inspector #19842
Client: John & Patricia Holden (Sellers)

============================================================
SECTION 1 — STRUCTURAL / FOUNDATION
============================================================
Observation: Minor mortar joint erosion on west foundation wall. Consistent
with normal settling for a home of this age (1987 construction).
Recommendation: Monitor annually; repoint if gap exceeds 3 mm.

============================================================
SECTION 2 — BASEMENT / CRAWLSPACE
============================================================
Observation: Water staining observed on concrete block walls along the north
and east perimeter of the basement. High-water mark lines visible at
approximately 4 inches above the floor slab on the north wall. Rust staining
on steel support column bases. Sellers disclosed during walkthrough that the
basement flooded in June 2019 and again in August 2021 following heavy rain
events; water accumulated to approximately 3–4 inches depth on both
occasions. A sump pump was installed after the 2019 event. No active moisture
was present at time of inspection.
Recommendation: Recommend full waterproofing evaluation by a licensed
waterproofing contractor. Disclose history to prospective buyers per Iowa
Code Section 558A.4.

============================================================
SECTION 3 — ROOF
============================================================
Observation: Asphalt shingle roof, original to 1987 construction. Estimated
remaining useful life: 3–5 years. Some granule loss in downslope sections.
No active leaks observed at time of inspection.
Recommendation: Anticipate roof replacement within 3–5 years. Disclose roof
age and condition to prospective buyers.

============================================================
SECTION 4 — ADDITIONS AND ALTERATIONS
============================================================
Observation: A room on the second floor (northwest bedroom) appears to have
been finished from an attic/bonus area. Ceiling follows roofline. Room
contains a closet and window but no separate HVAC supply vent. Sellers
confirmed during walkthrough that this room was added by a prior owner
in approximately 2018 and that no building permit was obtained for the
conversion. Cedar Falls building records do not reflect a permit for this
work.
Recommendation: Sellers are advised to disclose unpermitted construction to
prospective buyers. Iowa Code Section 558A.4 requires disclosure of known
material defects including unpermitted alterations. Buyer should be advised
that the unpermitted room may require retroactive permitting or may not
comply with current building codes.

============================================================
SECTION 5 — ELECTRICAL
============================================================
Observation: Panel upgraded 2015 to 200A service. All circuits tested
functional. GFCI protection present in kitchen and bathrooms.
Recommendation: No action required.
