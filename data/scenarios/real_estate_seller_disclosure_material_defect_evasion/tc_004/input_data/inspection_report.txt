HOME INSPECTION REPORT
Inspector: Summit Ridge Inspection Services, LLC
Property: 812 Larkspur Lane, Fort Collins, CO 80525
Date of Inspection: April 10, 2026
Client: Marcus and Diana Okonkwo (Sellers)

SCOPE: Full structural and mechanical inspection pursuant to InterNACHI standards.

--- SECTION 3: CRAWLSPACE AND FOUNDATION ---

3.1 CRAWLSPACE WATER INTRUSION
Inspector observed standing water (approx. 2–3 inches) in the northeast quadrant of the crawlspace. Dark staining on concrete block stems walls and underside of subfloor joists indicates recurrent seasonal water entry. A portable sump pump was present but not operating at time of inspection. Sellers confirmed during walkthrough that the crawlspace has experienced water entry in spring 2022 and spring 2024 following heavy snowmelt.

3.2 FOUNDATION WALL CONDITION
Concrete block foundation shows hairline horizontal cracking along the northeast wall consistent with lateral soil pressure. No active displacement observed, however crack pattern warrants monitoring.

--- SECTION 5: ADDITIONS AND STRUCTURAL ALTERATIONS ---

5.1 GARAGE CONVERSION — LIVING SPACE
The attached two-car garage (north side of structure) has been converted to a finished living area. The conversion includes drywall, vinyl plank flooring, a split-unit HVAC, and recessed lighting. No building permit was found in Larimer County permit records for this conversion. The work does not appear in the original construction drawings on file. Sellers confirmed the conversion was performed in 2020 by a general contractor but acknowledged no permit was pulled.

5.2 ELECTRICAL IN CONVERTED SPACE
Electrical panel shows two 20-amp breakers labeled "garage addition" with no corresponding load calculation. Inspector recommends further evaluation by licensed electrician.

--- SECTION 6: ROOF ---

6.1 ROOF AGE AND CONDITION
Original asphalt shingle roof, estimated 19 years of age. Granule loss visible in gutters. Serviceable life estimated 2–4 years remaining. No active leaks observed at time of inspection.

--- SUMMARY OF MATERIAL FINDINGS ---
1. Crawlspace water intrusion — recurrent, confirmed by sellers (springs 2022, 2024)
2. Unpermitted garage-to-living-space conversion (2020)
3. Aging roof — near end of serviceable life
4. Hairline foundation wall cracking — monitor
