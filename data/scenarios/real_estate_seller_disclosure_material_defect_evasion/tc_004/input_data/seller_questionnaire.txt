SELLER PROPERTY DISCLOSURE QUESTIONNAIRE
Property: 812 Larkspur Lane, Fort Collins, CO 80525
Sellers: Marcus Okonkwo and Diana Okonkwo
Date Completed: April 11, 2026

Q1. Are you aware of any past or present water intrusion, flooding, or moisture problems in the basement, crawlspace, or lower levels of the home?
A: Yes. We have had water come into the crawlspace in the spring of 2022 and again in spring 2024. It is seasonal snowmelt-related. We run a portable sump pump during those periods. We have not had the source waterproofed.

Q2. Are there any additions, structural alterations, or improvements that were made without required building permits?
A: Yes. We converted the attached garage to a living room and bedroom area in 2020. We hired a contractor who did the work but we did not obtain a permit from Larimer County. We are not aware of a final inspection having been done.

Q3. Are you aware of any roof defects, material damage, or known issues with the roof?
A: The inspector told us the roof is approximately 19 years old and may need replacement in the next few years. We have not had any active leaks while we lived here.

Q4. Are you aware of any foundation cracks, movement, or structural defects?
A: The inspector noted some hairline cracks on the northeast wall. We were not aware of them before the inspection. No movement has been observed.

Q5. Any other known material defects not covered above?
A: None that we are aware of.
