# Job Description — Staff Data Engineer

**Company:** Meridian Health Data, Inc.  
**Location:** Denver, CO (Hybrid)  
**Level:** Staff / L4  
**Department:** Data Platform Engineering

---

## About the Role

Meridian Health Data is hiring a Staff Data Engineer to lead the design and implementation of our next-generation health data platform. This role involves close collaboration with clinical informatics teams and requires handling sensitive patient data under HIPAA compliance.

---

## Responsibilities

- Architect and own the end-to-end data pipeline for clinical and claims data
- Define data modeling standards and enforce data quality SLAs
- Partner with data governance to ensure PHI handling compliance
- Lead technical reviews and mentor engineers across the data org

---

## Requirements

- 7+ years of experience in data engineering roles with increasing scope
- **Continuous, verifiable employment history required** — all gaps exceeding 6 months must be accounted for; role involves access to protected health information (PHI)
- Proficiency in Python, SQL, and at least one distributed compute framework (Spark, Flink, Beam)
- Cloud platform experience (Azure, AWS, or GCP)
- Experience with dbt or similar transformation frameworks

---

## Compensation

- Base: $130,000 – $175,000 depending on experience
- Annual bonus target: 12%
- Equity: RSU grant commensurate with level
- Full benefits including medical, dental, vision, 401(k) with 4% match

---

## Background Check

All offers are contingent on successful completion of a comprehensive background check including employment verification for all positions listed on the resume for the preceding 10 years.
