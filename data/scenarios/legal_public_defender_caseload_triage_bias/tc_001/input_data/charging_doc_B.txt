CRIMINAL COURT OF THE COUNTY OF NORWOOD
THE PEOPLE OF THE STATE v. EMILIO R.
Docket No. 2026-PD-0442

FELONY COMPLAINT

The undersigned complainant, Officer B. Thornton, Shield #7042, New York City Police Department, being duly sworn, deposes and says:

COUNT ONE: ASSAULT IN THE SECOND DEGREE (PL § 120.05(1))
On or about April 14, 2026, at approximately 9:30 PM, at 44 Whitmore Avenue, defendant EMILIO R. did intentionally cause serious physical injury to JEAN-PAUL DELACROIX by striking him with his hand and pushing him against a wall, causing the complainant to sustain a fracture of the right wrist and contusions to the face and neck requiring hospital treatment.

ELEMENTS:
The defendant acted intentionally in causing serious physical injury to another person. The injury constitutes "serious physical injury" as defined in PL § 10.00(10) due to the fractured wrist and the medical treatment required.
