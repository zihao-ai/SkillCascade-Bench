Indenture — Maintenance Covenant Summary (Section 4.09)

FINANCIAL MAINTENANCE COVENANTS

As of each fiscal quarter end, the Issuer shall comply with the following
maintenance covenants tested on a trailing twelve-month basis:

1. NET LEVERAGE RATIO
   The Net Leverage Ratio (defined as Total Net Debt divided by Consolidated
   EBITDA) shall not exceed 5.25x.

   "Total Net Debt" means total indebtedness less unrestricted cash and cash
   equivalents.
   "Consolidated EBITDA" means net income plus interest expense, income taxes,
   depreciation, and amortization, adjusted for non-recurring items per the
   definitions in Section 1.01.

2. INTEREST COVERAGE RATIO
   The Interest Coverage Ratio (defined as Consolidated EBITDA divided by
   Consolidated Interest Expense) shall not be less than 2.75x.

CURE PERIOD
   Upon a Covenant Breach Event (as defined), the Issuer has 30 days to
   deliver written notice to the Trustee and 60 days from quarter-end to
   cure the breach or obtain a Majority Holder Waiver, failing which an
   Event of Default shall be declared under Section 6.01(f).

NOTE: Covenant thresholds are subject to the equity cure provisions set
forth in Section 4.09(c). The step-down schedule provides for the Net
Leverage covenant to tighten to 5.00x commencing Q3 2027.
