RIVERSIDE CITY COUNCIL REGULAR MEETING
April 10, 2026 — Meeting Transcript (excerpt)

AGENDA ITEM 7: Infrastructure Maintenance Budget FY2026

COUNCIL MEMBER PATRICIA HAYES (Ward 3):
Thank you, Madam Chair. I want to address the allocation figures on page twelve of the budget appendix. The current proposal sets aside two point four million dollars for road resurfacing. Our public works department has documented that forty-three percent of Ward Three roads are rated poor or very poor on the standard PCI scale. At that funding level, we can resurface approximately eighteen lane-miles per year. Given that we have over two hundred lane-miles in substandard condition, the backlog would take more than eleven years to clear at this pace. I am asking the council to consider increasing the allocation to three point eight million to accelerate the timeline to seven years. This is consistent with the city engineer's own recommendation in the February 2026 infrastructure assessment report.

COUNCIL MEMBER DAVID OLSEN (Ward 1):
Thank you. The numbers Councilmember Hayes cited are from last year's assessment. The February 2026 report actually uses updated PCI thresholds following the ASTM revision, so the comparable figure is thirty-seven percent, not forty-three percent. I support additional investment but want to make sure we are debating the accurate baseline.

COUNCIL MEMBER PATRICIA HAYES (Ward 3):
I appreciate the clarification on the threshold revision. The figure in my briefing note was drawn from Table 4B of the February 2026 report, which I have here. The table heading states, quote, forty-three percent of lane-miles rated poor or very poor under revised thresholds. I am happy to submit it for the record so staff can reconcile the discrepancy.

CITY CLERK:
So noted. The document will be entered as Exhibit 7-A.
